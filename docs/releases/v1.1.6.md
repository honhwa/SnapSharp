# NimCap 截图工具 v1.1.6

- 区域截屏在选定范围后自动识别其中的二维码。识别在本地进行，普通文本会自动复制到剪贴板。
- 二维码包含 HTTP 或 HTTPS 网址时，界面会显示网址并询问是否用默认浏览器打开。选「是」会复制内容、退出截屏并打开网址。选「否」会提示已复制，并保留选区和标注以便继续操作。
- 全屏和活动窗口截屏也会在捕获后识别二维码。

下载 `nimcap-screenshot-v1.1.6-win-x64.zip`，解压完整压缩包后运行 `NimCap.Screenshot.exe`。压缩包包含 .NET 运行时，SHA-256 校验值见 `SHA256SUMS.txt`。

## English

- Region capture now scans for QR codes after you select an area. Scanning runs locally, and plain-text QR content is copied automatically.
- For HTTP or HTTPS links, the dialog shows the address and asks whether to open it in the default browser. Choose **Yes** to copy the content, exit capture, and open the link. Choose **No** to copy the content and continue working with the same selection and annotations.
- Full-screen and active-window captures also scan for QR codes after capture.

Download `nimcap-screenshot-v1.1.6-win-x64.zip`, extract the full archive, and launch `NimCap.Screenshot.exe`. The archive includes the .NET runtime. Verify it against `SHA256SUMS.txt`.
